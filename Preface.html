Preface

The recent publication of the world report on vision by the World Health Organization in 2019, followed by the publication of the Lancet commission on global health have all brought a renewed focus on eye care. These publications have also highlighted the vast disparities at national level, both in the capacity of eye care services and their availability across geographies within the countries. Also, for the first time, WHO has proposed, visual acuity of < 6/12 as the thresholds for intervention cataract surgery and refractive error. The flipside of all of these is that the eye care infrastructure of eye hospitals and primary eye care centres (Vision Centres) would have to increase multiple times, across the developing countries. Tens of thousands of vision centres and eye hospitals will need to come up to provide easy access and have the capacity to provide eye care to all. While many factors contribute to effective eye care, well-designed physical facility is an important factor as it can bring about efficiency and enhance patient experience. This highlighted a need and an opportunity for high-level guidance and considerations on the design of eye care facilities. This document is an offering towards this.
